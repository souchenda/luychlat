Privacy policy and terms of use

Updated: 27 October 2026

What we store

  • Your account: the phone number, email, Google or Telegram account you sign in with, and the name you set.
  • The financial records you enter: wallets, transactions, debts, budgets, goals, invoices, and the receipt or loan document photos you upload.
  • Location, only when you allow it, to calculate prayer times or find nearby ATMs.
  • Your PIN and Face ID / fingerprint stay on your device only and are never sent to our servers.

How it is protected

  • All connections use HTTPS (encrypted). Data is stored in a Supabase database that is encrypted at rest.
  • Database security rules make sure only you, and the members you invite to a shared account, can see your data.
  • Business and personal data are kept apart. Personal settings (such as Islamic Mode) are never shown to family members.
  • The Telegram bot never shows balances unless you turn on «Let AI see my numbers», and never in a group.
  • We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Services we use

  • Supabase — the database and sign-in.
  • Telegram — the bot and notifications, when you link Telegram.
  • AI providers (such as Google Gemini or Anthropic) — only when you use the AI Advisor or have a receipt photo read.
  • Cloudflare — protects the website and delivers it fast.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your financial data and files are deleted permanently.

AI Advisor

  • When you ask the AI Advisor, only a numeric summary (no names or phone numbers) is sent to the AI provider.
  • AI answers are general guidance, not professional financial advice.

PRO / ULTRA payments

  • Paid by KHQR. The app never stores your card details.

Your rights

  • Download a backup of your data in Settings › Data.
  • Delete your account and all its data permanently, from inside the app: how to delete your account

Terms of use

  • App Luy Chlat is a record-keeping and calculation tool. Its calculations (interest, Zakat, credit score, tax…) are for reference only.
  • You are responsible for the accuracy of what you enter and for keeping your PIN and password safe.
  • Do not use the app for unlawful activity. We may suspend accounts that break these terms.

Contact

Privacy questions: contact the support team on Telegram, @luychlat_support · info@luychlat.com
