Delete your App Luy Chlat account

You can delete your account and all its data yourself, from inside the app.

How to delete

  1. Sign in to the app (Android or app.luychlat.com).
  2. Open Settings and scroll down to «Danger zone».
  3. Tap «Delete account», read what will be deleted and tick «I understand».
  4. Enter your password, then tap «Delete my account permanently».

What is permanently deleted

  • All wallets, transactions, debts, budgets and goals
  • Your personal and business workspaces
  • Receipts, photos and loan documents
  • Any remaining PRO / ULTRA period (not refunded) and the Telegram link

This cannot be undone. Want to keep your data? Download a backup in Settings › Data first.

Can't sign in, or no password?

For an account signed in with Google or Telegram with no password, contact the support team on Telegram, @luychlat_support or email info@luychlat.com to ask for the deletion.
