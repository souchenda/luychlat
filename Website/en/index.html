Your money stays private

A money app has to earn your trust. Here's our promise.

App Luy Chlat never:

  • Touches your bank account — it only records and reminds.
  • Asks for your bank PIN or OTP. Anyone who does isn't us.
  • Sells your data or uses it for ads.
  • Charges you automatically.
  • Shows your balances in a Telegram group.

You're in control:

  • Balances hidden (*****) on open — tap to see.
  • App Lock with a 6-digit code, Face ID or fingerprint.

  • One account, one device at a time.
  • Business and personal, always apart.
  • Encrypted data — only you and the people you invite can see it.
  • Export your data or delete your account, any time.

When something goes wrong, we're ready:

  • Updates rarely interrupt the app — bigger ones happen at night.
  • The system is monitored automatically, day and night. If something goes wrong, you see a notice in the app at once — no need to ask.
  • A support code: we instantly know your app version and phone — never your money.

Short money tips

From the app's tip library — saving, paying off debt, running a shop

Build an emergency fund

Aim for 3–6 months of essential expenses, kept separate. Start small — even $10 a week adds up.

Give savings a name

Saving for “school fees” or “a new motorbike” is easier than “general savings”. Create separate goals and watch them grow.

Know a loan's true cost

2% a month is about 24% a year. Use the loan calculator before borrowing to see the total interest.

Lend with a record

Even when lending to family, note the amount, date and repayment date. It protects the relationship and makes polite reminders easy.

Separate shop and home money

Use separate wallets for the business. When you take shop money for yourself, record it as an owner's draw to see the real profit.

Watch cash flow daily

Profit on paper isn't cash in hand. Check money in, money out and who owes you, every day.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes — FREE is free forever. PRO and ULTRA are for those who need more, paid by KHQR.

Is App Luy Chlat a bank or an e-wallet? Can I withdraw money?

No. App Luy Chlat is a money notebook. Your money stays in your bank or your pocket — we never hold, withdraw or transfer it. To withdraw or send money, use your bank's app.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. Open app.luychlat.com in Safari, then Share › Add to Home Screen.

Is my data safe?

Your data is encrypted, and only you and the members you invite can see it. Your PIN and Face ID never leave your phone.

Does the SoundBox announce payments out?

No — money in only, and it respects your phone's Silent and Vibrate.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings › Delete account. More on the account deletion page.
