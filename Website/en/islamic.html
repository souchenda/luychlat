A day with Islamic Mode

  1. Fajr — the Adhan wakes you

    The Adhan sounds at your local prayer times, even with the screen locked or the app closed (Android). It respects your phone's Silent mode.

  2. On the road — Qibla and mosques

    The Qibla from wherever you are, and a map of mosques nearby.

  3. During the day — a du'a for now

    The right du'a for the moment, in Arabic with a Khmer reading and meaning — plus a tasbih counter.

  4. Maghrib — breaking the fast

    Imsak and Iftar times for where you are, whenever you fast.

  5. At night — the Quran

    Listen to every surah, download to listen offline, and nasheeds too.

Halal money

Zakat from the money you actually have

No more adding it up by hand — the app works it out from what you've recorded in your wallets.

Zakat at 2.5%

Cash, bank, gold and investments from your wallets, against the nisab (85 g of gold, or 595 g of silver).

The 354-day hawl

Note the day your wealth reached the nisab; the app tells you when Zakat is due.

Purifying interest (Riba)

Bank interest is kept apart, never counted as income — and you see what's left to give away.

Sadaqah and giving

Log the Zakat you've paid, sadaqah and waqf — with a yearly total.

Follows your madhab

Nisab by gold or silver; count receivables and short-term debts, or not.

Hijri calendar

The Hijri date shown beside the Gregorian one.

Private — only you see it. Family members never see this setting.

Turn the Adhan on or off on each phone — the shop phone stays quiet when the home phone calls the Adhan.

Greeted with «السلام عليكم», and the title Haji / Hajjah if you choose it.

Every du'a cites its source — the Quran or the hadith.
