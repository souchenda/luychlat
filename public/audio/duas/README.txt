Du'a recordings to listen to and memorise («ទន្ទេញ», lib/dua-audio.ts). Only licensed recordings: a local Imam's or
teacher's, recorded with written permission, or another source cleared for public commercial use (CLAUDE.md
Licensing). Name each file by the du'a's id in lib/duas.ts (wake.mp3, leave-home.mp3, before-eating.mp3,
after-eating.mp3, halal-provision.mp3, worry-debt.mp3, iftar.mp3, both-worlds.mp3, sleep.mp3) and add one line to
DUA_RECORDINGS with who recited it; the 🔊 button then appears on that du'a, and it plays offline once heard.
